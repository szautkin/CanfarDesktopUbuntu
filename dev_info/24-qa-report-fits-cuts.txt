1. FEATURE UNDER TEST
---------------------
New "min max cut out and cut levels" = three presets + dual control modes:
 - cutPreset: zscale (IRAF/DS9), percentile (p0.5-p99.5), minmax (dataMin-dataMax) — per set_fits_view:cutPreset enum
 - minCut/maxCut: physical BUNIT values (direct display window)
 - minCutPercentile/maxCutPercentile: percentile 0-100 scale-free window
Wording from tool: "zscale is the IRAF/DS9 default and usually the right choice: a percentile cut asks where most pixels are, which is the wrong question for faint structure under a few bright stars."
